# Two-beat finale: last-call standings + freeze + podium (`d15-finale`)

Implements `plans/daily-cards-spec.md` § "Scoring and social surfaces" → "The finale—two-beat finish" and § "Farewell view". The Event ends on two scheduled beats, timed by `finaleTimes` (`d15-scheduler-unlock`, #202): a last-call Moment posts the going-into-the-final-night standings at `lastCallAt`, and at the closing Day's own `unlockAt` the standings **freeze** (`EventDoc.frozenAt` set), the closing Day unlocks, and the closing view opens with the **podium**—Event champion, Event-wide First to BINGO, and the ten daily honors—also posted as a final Moment. In the STANDARD shape those instants are **20:00 on Day 9** and **08:00 on Day 10**; a schedule whose closing Day would otherwise leave an empty last-call window instead derives `lastCallAt` backwards from the close (#784), so this ticket's content functions take the boundary from `finaleTimes` rather than assuming a fixed wall-clock time. `d15-scheduler-unlock` (#202) owns firing the two beats on schedule; this ticket owns their CONTENT—the standings/podium copy and computation—and the client-side farewell podium banner + freeze semantics.

## Contract

- `functions/src/finaleContent.ts` (new, needs-phase-4)—pure, injectable content functions the scheduler's last-call / freeze+podium triggers call into (the STANDARD shape fires those at 20:00-D9 / 08:00-D10; #784's same-date fallback fires last-call earlier, relative to the close, instead). No `firebase-admin`, no live backend (mirrors `unlockDay.ts`'s decoupled-pure posture):
  - `lastCallStandingsCopy(players, opts?)`—the going-into-the-final-night line ("Jess leads by 2 bingos—standings freeze at 8 a.m."), naming the leader and their margin; degrades to a generic line on a tie or an empty board. `opts.freezePhrase` overrides the trailing "standings freeze at …" clause; `DEFAULT_FREEZE_PHRASE` ("standings freeze at 8 a.m") is only the fallback for a schedule with no usable closing-Day unlock, never the normal case (#800 — see `freezePhraseForUnlock` below).
  - `freezePhraseForUnlock(farewellUnlockAt, timeZone)` (#800)—derives the ACTUAL freeze-phrase from the closing Day's own `unlockAt`, formatted in the Event's timezone ("standings freeze at 11 a.m" for an 11:00 closing Day, not a hardcoded 8). `runFinaleBeats` (`functions/src/unlockDay.ts`) computes this once per last-call beat, passes it as `lastCallStandingsCopy`'s `opts.freezePhrase`, and persists it on the posted Moment (`lastCall.freezePhrase`) so the client's reconstruction reads the SAME string rather than reformatting the instant itself. Falls back to `DEFAULT_FREEZE_PHRASE` when `farewellUnlockAt` is missing/non-finite.
  - `normalizeTimezone(raw)` / `DEFAULT_TIMEZONE` (#800 Codex P2)—mirrors `src/data/converters.ts`'s `normalizeTimezone`/`DEFAULT_TIMEZONE` exactly (`Europe/Rome`), restated here because `runFinaleBeats` reads the raw Firestore Event doc directly, bypassing `eventConverter`. A malformed/missing `timezone` must resolve to the SAME zone on both sides, or the freeze phrase reintroduces the very two-rendering-paths-disagree bug #800 exists to close, one layer down.
  - `buildPodiumPayload(players, days, dayHonors)`—the podium payload: Event champion (top of the aggregated standings, EXCLUDING the ceremonial closing Day), Event-wide First to BINGO (non-Tutorial Days only—a Tutorial Day's mark never takes the honor). Excluded by `DayDef.tutorial` ALONE, never by pool: pool identity and Tutorial framing are independent (ADR 0011), and a curated pool can be real competitive play, and the ten daily honors read from each Day's `meta.firstBingo`.
  - The ranking + tutorial-exclusion semantics MIRROR `src/game/logic.ts`'s `comparePlayers` / `eventFirstBingoAt` (the app and functions packages are deliberately decoupled, like `autohide.ts` from `moderation.ts`).
- `src/data/finale.ts` (new)—the client mirror:
  - `buildPodium(players, days)`—the same podium the scheduler posts, computed client-side from the live-but-frozen `PlayerDoc` aggregates + per-Day honors. The champion/standings EXCLUDE the closing Day's contribution, so post-freeze closing-Day marks never move the podium (the closing-Day-is-ceremonial rule; the podium is "as of `frozenAt`", not live).
  - `farewellPinIndex(days, frozenAt, now)`—the default-view pin: the closing Day's array index once `frozenAt` is set AND the closing Day is unlocked, else `null` (fall back to the normal "today" default). Never pins before the freeze.
- `src/lastCallCopy.ts` (new, #800; extracted from `src/components/ProofFeed.tsx`)—`lastCallLineFromPlayers(players, freezePhrase?)`, the client's ban-aware reconstruction of the last-call line: `ProofFeed.tsx` cannot simply display the scheduler's posted `line` string, because a Player banned AFTER the beat posted must still be removable from a still-live leaderboard read, so the Feed re-derives the sentence from the Moment's `lastCall.players`, filtered to exclude any now-banned uid. It takes `moment.lastCall.freezePhrase` as its freeze-phrase argument (falling back to the historical literal only for a legacy Moment posted before #800) rather than an independently hardcoded string, mirroring `lastCallStandingsCopy`'s leader/margin phrasing (a parity test, `tests/functions/lastcall-copy-parity.test.ts`, pins the two together).
- `src/components/FarewellPodium.tsx` (new)—the closing view's podium banner (champion + Event-wide First to BINGO + ten daily-honor rows), rendered ABOVE the goodbye banner (`d15-tutorial-banners` owns the goodbye copy/mount; this ticket owns the podium banner and their stacking order). Issue #449 later added the podium's own share affordance—a "Share final standings" button at the bottom of the section rendering the same frozen `Podium` payload as an on-device Share Card; the card and share mechanics are owned by `specs/w2-share-cards.md` § Final standings.
- `src/components/Board.tsx` (modify)—mount the podium above the goodbye banner on the closing Day once frozen, and pin the closing Day as the default view once the Event has ended (`farewellPinIndex`).
- `src/types.ts` / `src/data/converters.ts`—`EventDoc.frozenAt?: number` (ms epoch) is the freeze stamp; a missing field reads as `undefined` (unset) through `eventConverter`'s passthrough. (Landed with the #212 schema contract.) `LastCallMomentPayload.freezePhrase?: string` (#800) is the scheduler's computed freeze phrase, persisted on the `last_call` Moment.

## Acceptance criteria

- **Given** `finaleTimes.lastCallAt` has arrived (20:00 on Day 9 in the standard shape; earlier, relative to the close, when #784's same-date fallback applies), **when** the scheduled trigger fires, **then** a last-call Moment posts naming the current leader and their margin. (Test: last-call-copy.)
- **Given** the closing Day's `unlockAt`, **when** the last-call Moment's freeze phrase is built, **then** it quotes the closing Day's ACTUAL unlock time formatted in the Event's timezone (not a hardcoded 8 a.m.), and the client's ban-aware reconstruction quotes the SAME phrase from the persisted Moment rather than an independently formatted one. (Test: freeze-phrase, #800.)
- **Given** the closing Day's own `unlockAt` has arrived (08:00 on Day 10 in the standard shape), **when** the scheduled trigger fires, **then** `frozenAt` is set, the closing Day unlocks, and a podium Moment posts with the champion, Event-wide First to BINGO, and ten daily honors. (Test: podium-payload.)
- **Given** the Event has ended (`frozenAt` set) and the closing Day is unlocked, **when** a Player opens the app, **then** the closing Day (podium included) is the default view; before the freeze it is never pinned. (Test: default-view-pin.)
- **Given** the podium, **then** its Event-wide First to BINGO never credits a Tutorial Day's mark. (Test: tutorial-exclusion.)
- **Given** the podium, **then** the champion/standings are computed EXCLUDING the closing Day, so a post-freeze closing-Day mark never changes the podium. (Test: ceremonial-farewell.)
- **Given** the event doc, **then** `frozenAt` is admin/Function-writable only, never client-writable directly by a Player. (Test: rules.)

## Test coverage

- `tests/functions/d15-finale.test.ts` (functions layer, `vitest.functions.config.ts`)—`lastCallStandingsCopy` names the correct leader + margin from a fixture player set and degrades on a tie/empty board; `freezePhraseForUnlock` covers on-the-hour/off-the-hour/p.m. formatting, the missing-timezone and invalid-unlock fallbacks, and the `Europe/Rome` legacy default (#800); `normalizeTimezone` covers offset ids, GMT/UTC/Etc aliases, bare abbreviations, and unresolvable zone ids (#800 Codex P2); `buildPodiumPayload` excludes a Tutorial Day's first bingo from the Event-wide honor, excludes the closing Day from the champion totals, and includes all ten daily honors when present.
- `tests/functions/d15-scheduler-unlock.test.ts`—`runFinaleBeats` posts a last-call Moment whose `line` and `lastCall.freezePhrase` quote a Bodega-shaped closing Day's ACTUAL 11:00 `America/Los_Angeles` unlock, not a hardcoded 8 a.m. (#800).
- `tests/functions/lastcall-copy-parity.test.ts` (#800, cf. `finale-parity.test.ts`)—feeds identical player/freeze-phrase fixtures to `lastCallStandingsCopy` (functions) and `lastCallLineFromPlayers` (client) and asserts byte-identical output.
- `tests/functions/timezone-normalize-parity.test.ts` (#800 Codex P2)—feeds identical raw `timezone` values to both packages' `normalizeTimezone` and asserts byte-identical output.
- `src/lastCallCopy.test.ts` (Vitest unit, #800)—`lastCallLineFromPlayers` defaults to the historical freeze phrase when none is given, uses an injected one otherwise, and degrades the same way `lastCallStandingsCopy` does on a tie/empty board.
- `src/components/FarewellPodium.test.tsx` (RTL/jsdom)—renders champion, Event-wide First to BINGO, and ten daily-honor rows from a fixture payload, above the goodbye banner mount point.
- `src/data/d15-finale.test.ts` (Vitest unit)—`farewellPinIndex` pins only once `frozenAt` is set and the closing Day is unlocked, never before; `buildPodium` picks the champion, excludes the ceremonial closing Day, and excludes tutorial Days from the Event-wide First to BINGO.
- `tests/rules/d15-finale.test.ts` (rules emulator)—`EventDoc.frozenAt` is writable by an admin/Function but rejected for a non-admin Player.
